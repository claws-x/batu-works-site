FAQ

Questions & answers

Can I download the apps?

Not yet. Daily Quotes and Element Atlas are in development. A verified download link will appear when a public release exists.

Who runs BATU WORKS?

Ben directs the company. AI tools assist with research, engineering and review; they do not replace human release decisions.

Is the AI development factory fully automated?

No. Some handoff and engineering steps were verified, but end-to-end unattended delivery and cross-provider recovery remain unproven.

How do you check quality?

We distinguish successful builds, independent review, actual device testing and release approval. Each is a separate gate.

Does the website track me?

We do not use first-party analytics, ad pixels, accounts or contact forms. The hosting provider may process technical logs.

How can I get support?

An official contact channel will be published before the apps are released.
