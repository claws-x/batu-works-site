PRODUCT PRACTICE

Problem → prototype → proof.

Automation is a means, not the product. We favor real user needs over complicated internal systems.

01 / Discover

Find a specific need

Understand the problem, audience and alternatives before committing to a build.

02 / Build

Keep the scope honest

Build a usable small version with reviewable changes and reproducible checks.

03 / Verify

Do not confuse build with launch

Check behavior, privacy, device experience and release readiness separately.
