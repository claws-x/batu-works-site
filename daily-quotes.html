Daily Quotes.

A small moment of perspective, every day. A quiet reading experience built to stay simple.

Follow developmentAll products
What it does

A tiny daily ritual.

Read a daily quote without a noisy feed. Choose a reminder time if you want a gentle nudge. Keep the experience focused and personal.

Current prototype scope

Small on purpose

  • Offline daily quote reading.
  • Optional local reminder at a chosen time.
  • Reminder settings retained across restarts.

Prototype functionality has been internally tested; public device validation and release are not complete.

Availability

Not in the App Store yet.

The first prototype has passed internal review. Device testing, signing and store submission are still required. We will not present it as downloadable until its public listing is verified.
